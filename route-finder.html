Shortest route finder

Find the shortest round trip that starts at one stop, visits every other stop once, and returns to the start.

1. Your stops

Use 2 to 8 stops.

2. Distances between stops

Enter the distance for each pair (any unit, such as km or miles). Going from A to B is treated the same as B to A.

How it works

  • A round trip starts at one stop and ends back at the same stop.
  • The tool uses brute force: it tries every possible order of visiting the stops, adds up the distances for each, and keeps the smallest total.
  • That is fine for a handful of stops, but the number of orders grows very fast, which is why real delivery companies use smarter methods for large routes.
